# FAQ

## Where should I ask a question?

Use [Discussions](https://github.com/embeddedos-org/{{repository}}/discussions)
for support and design questions. Open an
[Issue](https://github.com/embeddedos-org/{{repository}}/issues/new/choose) when
the work is concrete and belongs to this repository.

## Where are supported platforms and commands documented?

Use the
[versioned documentation source](https://github.com/embeddedos-org/{{repository}}/blob/{{default_branch}}/{{documentation_source}}).
The Wiki does not invent support claims or commands.

## How do I report a vulnerability?

Use the private route in the
[security policy](https://github.com/embeddedos-org/{{repository}}/security/policy),
never a public issue.

## How do I find related components?

Browse the
[EmbeddedOS organization repositories](https://github.com/orgs/embeddedos-org/repositories)
and the [organization profile](https://github.com/embeddedos-org).
